What does the next kilowatt-hour cause?

The attributional factor (AEF) spreads all emissions across all electricity. The marginal factor (MEF) answers a different question: which power plant responds when consumption rises by one kWh — and what does that plant emit.

Why the explanatory variable decides everything

Each point is one hour-to-hour transition. Against total generation there is no relationship, because generation rises mainly with the sun and solar is emission-free. Against residual load — demand after wind and solar have fed in — the slope of that line is the marginal emission factor.

Which power plant actually responds

Share of hours in which each dispatchable generator moved most strongly in the direction of the change in residual load. Pumped storage is excluded: it does not produce energy, it shifts it.
